# Game Preservation
As newer games come out every year what happens to the older games? They used to be forgotten about until the newest system came out and a classics game was released. 
Game presentationis about keeping old games, their hardware, and files alive for future generations to play and study them. 
Here is a very good source that has a ton of game presentation sites on it! [Learn More Here](https://preserve.games/#browse)
